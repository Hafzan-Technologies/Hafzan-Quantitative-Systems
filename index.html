Hafzan Composite Adaptive Valuation Model (HCAVM) Fair Value Estimator

The Hafzan Composite Adaptive Valuation Model (HCAVM) is an advanced framework for estimating the fair value of startup shares in private and secondary markets. It combines traditional methods (like comparables and DCF) with modern enhancements including probability-weighted exits, real options theory, liquidity discounts, and market sentiment multipliers. Use this interactive calculator to explore how investor assumptions impact valuation in venture capital and private equity contexts.
